Frequently asked questions

  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen tap «نسيت كلمة المرور» and enter your number; the Basak team sends you a recovery code.
  • Receipt rejected: the reason is shown on the subscription page. Upload a new receipt from the same page.
  • No line for my university: only lines that serve your university are shown. Contact a bus company in your area, or write to us.
  • Not receiving notifications: make sure notifications for Basak are allowed in your phone settings, then open the app once while online.
  • Delete your account: Account → Delete account. Details on the delete account page.
